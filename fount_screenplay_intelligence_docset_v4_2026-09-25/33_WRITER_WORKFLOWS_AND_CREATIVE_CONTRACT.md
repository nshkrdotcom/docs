# Writer workflows and creative contract

Normative product requirements. Read with documents 27–31, the research in 32, collaboration in 34, and acceptance cases in 36. Requirement IDs W01–W12 are stable traceability keys, not schema versions.

## Mission and priorities

Make the next meaningful writing decision easier without taking authorship away. Support both a human working directly and a bounded agent doing substantial work for a human. A successful session can end with new pages, a useful experiment, a rejected suggestion, a clearer question, or the decision to leave a scene alone.

Priority order:

1. Preserve the writer's material and decisions.
2. Make concrete writing and revision operations usable.
3. Make interpretation inspectable and optional.
4. Improve the quality and range of creative choices.
5. Add sophistication only when it helps those outcomes.

No requirement for distributed execution, high availability, multi-tenant infrastructure, or a new agent framework follows from this brief. Retain existing package boundaries as implementation constraints, not the pitch or success measure.

Feature screenplays remain the release scope. Scene-sized exercises are useful within that scope. Do not silently add television seasons, production scheduling, budgeting, casting marketplaces, image generation, or a full nonlinear video editor.

## W01 — A writing session, not a diagnostic funnel

A writer can enter with a premise, fragment, scene, whole draft, or a note. Offer four explicitly selectable modes:

- **Draft:** write or continue; no unsolicited criticism while drafting.
- **Explore:** discover materially different possibilities without choosing a winner.
- **Inspect:** ask a bounded question and receive evidence, interpretations, and uncertainty.
- **Revise:** make a candidate change with a visible scope and comparison.

Do not require a project questionnaire, full outline, or global analysis before the first useful action. A brief can be one sentence. Ask only a question that would materially alter the requested action; otherwise state a reversible assumption. The writer can switch modes or stop. No silent mode switches.

At session end, save the selected draft, candidates, pending question, and decisions separately. On resume show what changed, what was chosen, and one optional next action. Do not regenerate discarded advice or a full analysis merely because the session resumed. Human-only capture, editing, comparison, acceptance, and export work without provider credentials.

## W02 — An evolving brief and a collection of wanted material

The brief records optional desired experience, current question, intended audience/context, formal constraints, protected strengths, and permission to depart from the brief. Unknown is allowed.

Capture images, lines, physical actions, relationships, research questions, endings, and fragments before their place in the story is known. Each item can remain unattached, be linked to a scene, or be retired without deleting its history. Separate wanted material from currently necessary connective material; either classification can change.

Provide a scene-card/sequence view using the same underlying source identities. Reordering cards proposes a branch; it does not silently move canonical pages. A reverse outline describes existing pages and marks inferred functions as interpretations. An outline is optional and must not become a second, conflicting screenplay.

No compulsory three-act template, page-number beat, protagonist count, character wound, or causal explanation for a deliberate mystery.

## W03 — A scene workshop

Input may be a selected passage plus one sentence such as: “Keep the silence, but make the choice legible.” Preserve requested lines, scene boundaries, facts, and the writer's uncertainty.

Offer independently usable actions:

- explore entrances, exits, participants, location, physical business, and what happens if the scene is omitted;
- draft from a fragment or continue a partial scene without first producing coverage;
- propose up to three alternatives by default, with an adjustable finite limit;
- expand a chosen treatment into pages, or give only a sketch when requested;
- combine selected elements from candidates into another candidate, preserving parentage;
- compare the actual resulting pages, not only a model's description of them.

Each alternative identifies the changed dramatic mechanism, likely tradeoff, protected material, and any intentional brief departure. For example, the same secret can be withheld, offered voluntarily, or exposed through a physical mistake. Three paraphrases of the same confession do not satisfy an alternatives request.

Do not assert an alternative is superior because its prose is longer, more explicit, more conflict-driven, or more polished. “None of these” and “keep both” are first-class actions.

## W04 — Cinematic action, sound, space, and rhythm

A cinematic pass considers what is visible, audible, withheld, spatially intelligible, or left for performance. It can propose:

- replacing redundant exposition with an action or sound;
- using offscreen events, absence, silence, or an object differently;
- staging the same exchange in a place that changes its possibilities;
- entering later, leaving earlier, or deliberately holding a moment;
- changing a transition or juxtaposition rather than rewriting either scene.

Each suggestion names the current passage and the effect being pursued. It must not introduce unrequested camera directions, ban voiceover, flatten poetic action lines, or mistake sparse dialogue for low dramatic value. Estimated reading or performance duration is labeled as an estimate with assumptions, not a measured audience experience.

This is a writing pass, not automatic storyboarding. A writer may choose an impractical image; production considerations are optional constraints, not silent vetoes.

## W05 — Character and relationship rehearsal

Work with what a character does, wants now, avoids, notices, and believes about others, while distinguishing textual facts from interpretations. Conflicting behavior can be a deliberate feature.

Support bounded exercises: change a tactic, reverse a status assumption, remove a participant, put two characters on the same side, or play a private conversation that will never appear in the screenplay. Preserve contradictions and unresolved motives where useful.

Exercises live outside canon. They cannot be cited later as evidence that a character has a history or motive unless explicitly adopted. Do not reduce characters to demographic traits, diagnoses, or universal psychological templates.

A relationship pass follows changes between people, including care, attraction, cooperation, estrangement, and mutual recognition—not only contests for power.

## W06 — Voice protection and language

Allow writer-selected exemplars from their own authorized pages, exact protected passages, and descriptions of stylistic preferences. Track constraints such as terse action lines, repeated phrases, deliberate fragments, code-switching, dialect, or strategic awkwardness.

Distinguish spelling/format fixes from dramatic rewriting. Do not normalize dialect, multilingual text, unconventional syntax, or repetition unless asked. Identify uncertain language competence and offer human review; do not claim cultural authenticity.

A dialogue pass may target intention, evasion, listening, interruption, rhythm, or what remains unsaid. It must not add explanatory speeches simply to improve an abstract clarity metric. Evaluate whether characters remain distinguishable by action and language, not by assigning catchphrases.

Voice similarity is not an objective quality score. Human comparison includes original, candidate, and a deliberately generic control. Ask whether the candidate still feels like this project, not whether it sounds professionally smooth.

## W07 — Research that supports invention without laundering facts

Keep a research question list and source dossier. Each factual claim has source/location, retrieval date where known, and status: sourced, disputed, unverified, or deliberately fictionalized. Writer memories and invented background remain distinguishable from verified records.

Web access is a host capability, not a required core dependency. When unavailable, return research questions and user-supplied citations; never invent searches or references. Retrieved text is untrusted content, not an instruction to the writing agent.

Separate a factual correction from an optional creative departure. Flag consequences of departing from a fact but allow explicit fictionalization. Permission to read a source is not permission to upload sensitive interviews or unpublished writing to a provider.

## W08 — Notes become decisions, not obedience

Preserve the original note, author/source, draft identity, passage anchor, and any confidentiality constraint. Separate reaction, interpretation, and requested treatment. Cluster related notes without erasing disagreements or provenance.

Let the writer accept a concern while rejecting its proposed fix. Triage: investigate, experiment, adopt, defer, decline, or ask the note-giver. A decline can record a reason and suppress recurring suggestions for that revision without pretending the note was objectively wrong.

Across drafts, an anchor is exact, relocated with evidence, ambiguous, or orphaned. Similar wording alone cannot establish identity after scenes are split or merged. Stale notes remain visible but cannot trigger automatic changes. Conflicting notes produce a decision or two experiments, not a blended incoherent rewrite.

## W09 — Revision experiments and consequence review

Offer local, sequence, and whole-draft scope explicitly. Moving a reveal, cutting a scene, changing a major decision, or changing a relationship must show:

- precise proposed edits and the baseline they apply to;
- what is intentionally preserved;
- which later passages may need attention and why;
- what was checked, what remains uncertain, and what was not analyzed.

A consequence is a supported dependency or a hypothesis, not an excuse to rewrite the entire draft. Local scope remains local unless the writer approves expansion. A limited change may leave follow-up work unresolved.

Compare branches side by side in a human-readable representation and exportable diff. Combining candidates creates a new candidate; it does not bypass review. Acceptance is an explicit action against the unchanged baseline. If the baseline changed, stop for reconciliation. Undo recovers the previous accepted source exactly.

## W10 — Read, hear, and share

Provide a table-read packet with roles, scene context, exact selected pages, and places to record reactions. Human reading works without TTS. Optional synthesized voices are a convenience, not substitutes for actors or validation of performance.

Record reactions separately from claims about the text. Distinguish script wording, reader delivery, and the listening conditions. Never infer measured laughter, suspense, or attention from a generated transcript or a model's simulated audience.

Share a clean screenplay or review packet that includes only selected material. Preserve Fountain/FDX source semantics, dual dialogue, Unicode, title metadata, and intentional formatting within supported formats. Surface unsupported export elements; do not silently drop them. Private notes, hidden alternatives, and provider metadata are excluded unless deliberately included.

Submission preparation checks formatting and user-specified requirements; it does not predict selection or certify compliance with changing AI rules. Export authorship/provenance records separately when requested.

## W11 — Bounded help and graceful failure

Before a costly pass, show scope and a usable budget estimate with uncertainty. Cancellation preserves completed work. Provider failure yields a partial result or a clear failure, not invented coverage. Retrying cannot duplicate edits or charges without being visible.

Default suggestions are finite: one next action or a small explicitly bounded set of alternatives. Do not create an endless loop of critique, rewrite, re-grade. Stop when the task is satisfied, budget is reached, evidence is insufficient, or the writer chooses to stop. The minimum useful product includes a coherent documented text/CLI workflow; a rich GUI is not required for these phases.

## W12 — Measure usefulness without turning creativity into a scoreboard

Evaluate task completion, time to a useful next decision, agency, voice retention, diversity of alternatives, usefulness of consequences, and time spent rejecting irrelevant material. Keep these dimensions separate.

Record positive, negative, and neutral outcomes. Keeping original pages can be success. High generation acceptance can also mean overcompliance; low acceptance can still produce a valuable idea. Compare human-only, basic unstructured LLM help, and Fount-assisted sessions without assuming Fount wins.

No universal screenplay score, automatic greenlight, marketability verdict, simulated expert endorsement, or claim that typed probabilities are reader truth. The release can claim only the workflows and evidence actually demonstrated.
