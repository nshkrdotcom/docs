# Phase 13 Optional Writer / Language Review Packet

**Status: NOT_RUN. Optional and nonblocking under D046.**

If explicitly commissioned, use only rights-cleared writer-owned material. Present, blind where practical, (1) the untouched scene, (2) one cinematic visual/stillness candidate, (3) one sound/space candidate, and (4) a deliberately fluent generic explanatory control. Preserve exact protected passages and any multilingual/dialect/clipped material.

Ask the writer to record separately: which version they prefer and why; whether silence/stillness/object behavior changed; whether any candidate explained emotion that the original left productively unresolved; whether repeated language, dialect, multilingual text or clipped action was normalized; whether the original remains stronger; and whether any language/cultural change requires a competent human reviewer.

For rehearsal, show the invented exercise separately from canon and ask whether the writer explicitly adopts, rejects, or leaves it as rehearsal-only. Record actor/note/time/source identifiers.

Do not aggregate this into a screenplay score, voice score, language-authenticity certification, population claim or model endorsement. A preference for the original is a valid result.
