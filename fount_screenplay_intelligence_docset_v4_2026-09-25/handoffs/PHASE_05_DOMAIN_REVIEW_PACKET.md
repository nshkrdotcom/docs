# Phase 5 diagnosis/playbook usefulness review packet

**Status:** optional protocol retained; not run in this source-writing pass. No reviewer, writer, usefulness or human-validity result is claimed.

## Goal

When a real study is desired, test whether Phase-5 diagnosis packets help an experienced screenwriter/story reviewer understand a reported screenplay problem without overclaiming certainty or prescribing a rewrite too aggressively.

## Suggested cases

Use rights-cleared feature screenplay material and concrete writer concerns. Include at least one case where two explanations plausibly fit the same concern, one case with meaningful counterevidence, and one case where missing evidence should cause abstention/next investigation rather than a forced diagnosis.

## Review dimensions

Keep source support/validity separate from creative usefulness. Reviewers may assess evidence traceability, clarity, alternative-awareness, respect for declared intent/protected strengths, uncertainty honesty, source navigability, usefulness to the next writing decision, and whether strategy language becomes too prescriptive.

## Required packet fields

Freeze the exact screenplay revision/selection, concern/intended effect/protected strengths, evidence IDs, model/provider/runtime identity for any model estimates, complete writer result packet, deterministic rendered form, resource usage, reviewer identity or pseudonymous study ID, rights/retention/export policy, and independent reviewer response before reconciliation.

## Completion rule

Under D046 this pilot is optional and skipped by default. Phase 5 may become `COMPLETE` on applicable engineering/QC evidence without it. Keep the unperformed study as visible validation debt and never describe fixtures, Sandbox output, Codex review or model output as human usefulness validation.
