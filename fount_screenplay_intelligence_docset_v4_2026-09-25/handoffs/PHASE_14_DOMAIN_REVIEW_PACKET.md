# Phase 14 Optional Collaborator / Writer Review Packet

**Status: NOT_RUN. Optional and nonblocking under D046.**

If explicitly commissioned, use writer-owned/authorized material and keep private research export permissions intact.

For **research**, show the writer a compact source dossier with claim status (`sourced`, `disputed`, `unverified`, `deliberately fictionalized`) and ask whether the factual/fictional distinction matches intent. Never ask reviewers to treat retrieved instructions as authority.

For **notes**, present conflicting raw notes separately with their source/original-draft anchors. Ask the writer to mark concern and proposed treatment independently, including “accept concern / reject treatment,” and to resolve any ambiguous/orphaned anchor manually rather than guessing.

For **consequential revision**, show the untouched base, the selected note-linked candidate, the actual page diff, declared supported dependencies, uncertain hypotheses, unresolved downstream work, and any unrelated changed scenes. Ask whether the declared scope respected the writer's intent and whether a follow-up experiment is warranted.

Record preferences/decisions as human judgments, not a screenplay score or model-quality endorsement. A decision to keep the original, decline the note, preserve uncertainty, or leave a downstream question unresolved is a valid result.
