# Optional Phase 8 domain/usefulness review packet

**Status:** `NOT_RUN`. Under D046 this review is optional, skipped by default, and never blocks engineering completion or later phases. No human participant, emotional-validity, genre-conformity, theme-authority, reader-agreement or writer-usefulness result is claimed.

If commissioned later, use rights-cleared feature-screenplay material and keep support/factuality separate from usefulness:

1. **Emotional / Value Movement** — compare a consequential scene run with an intentionally restrained/steadfast run. Ask whether the reported condition/value transitions are source-supported and useful without claiming universal emotion.
2. **Theme / Meaning** — use a feature with at least two plausible value-conflict readings. Ask whether competing hypotheses retain meaningful support and counterevidence rather than converging on one “true theme.”
3. **Genre Packs** — test at least one hybrid/custom project pack and one deliberate subversion/anti-genre instruction. Confirm the pack changes salience/questions without turning conventions into mandatory rules.
4. **Revision Intelligence** — compare a real before/after revision made for a stated concern with explicit protected strengths. Ask whether target effect, collateral changes, reader/character/relationship trajectories, causal ripple and story-time continuity are separated usefully.

Record disagreements and non-findings. Do not create an overall screenplay score, a best-strategy verdict, or a claim that the system predicts audience response.
