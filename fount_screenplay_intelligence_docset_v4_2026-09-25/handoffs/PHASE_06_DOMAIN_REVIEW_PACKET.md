**Status:** optional protocol retained; not run in this source-writing pass. No reviewer, writer-usefulness, calibration or human-validity result is claimed.

## Goal

If a real study is commissioned, test whether the four Phase-6 capability packets help a feature-screenplay writer understand scene function, agency/causality, character trajectory and relationship movement without forcing a formula or misreading non-linear presentation.

## Suggested rights-cleared cases

Use author-owned, licensed or otherwise rights-cleared feature screenplay material. Include at least one non-linear case where a relationship/character event is presented later than its diegetic position, plus cases with deliberate stasis/reactivity so reviewers can detect over-prescription.

## Separate ratings

Keep these independent:

- factual/source support of each claim;
- evidence citation correctness;
- distinction between presentation order, story time and causality;
- preservation of writer intent/protected strengths;
- usefulness for deciding what to investigate or revise;
- uncertainty honesty / abstention quality;
- over-prescriptiveness;
- false-positive pressure on deliberate stasis, reactive protagonists or non-transformational arcs.

## Required record if run

Record corpus rights/privacy metadata, exact source revision, reviewer role, packet identity, per-claim support decisions, usefulness responses, disagreements and reconciliation. Do not convert model confidence into reader agreement.
