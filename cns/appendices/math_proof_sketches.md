# Appendix — Proof Sketches

## Zero-temperature soundness

For monotone Horn rules over finite evidence atoms, zero-temperature activation is deterministic. Forward closure derives heads only when all body atoms are present. By induction on derivation depth, every derived atom has a finite proof tree with leaves in evidence atoms.

## Grounding conservation

If macro coherence increases only when micro coherence increases at least as fast, and if micro coherence initially dominates macro coherence, then macro coherence cannot overtake micro coherence under allowed updates.

## Orthesis fixed point

If `G ∘ S` is contractive under the chosen logic-space metric, Banach's fixed point theorem gives a unique fixed point. Practical systems should test empirical contraction by measuring round-trip chirality across iterations.

## Residual decomposition utility

If residual tensor factorization reduces held-out contradiction energy while increasing complexity by less than an MDL penalty, the latent context predicate is useful. Otherwise it is spurious and rejected.
