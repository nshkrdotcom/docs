# 13 — Glossary

**CNS:** Chiral Narrative Synthesis.

**GCTS:** Grounded Chiral Tensor Synthesis.

**Likely-truth ranking:** A calibrated posterior score over structured possible worlds estimating how likely a claim is under available evidence, access states, source reliability, contradiction structure, and model calibration.

**Strict proof support:** Posterior mass of worlds where a claim appears in zero-temperature proof closure with valid evidence references and proof traces.

**Evidence atom:** Immutable evidence span, observation, record, or datum with provenance, source quality, and access path.

**Record-access state:** Structured representation of whether an expected record is available, inaccessible, sealed, withheld, destroyed, not generated, or unknown.

**Record-generation duty:** Estimate that a record should exist under ordinary policy, instrumentation, practice, role obligation, or procedure.

**Evidence of absence:** A record or observation expected to reveal a fact exists and affirmatively negates that fact.

**Absence of evidence:** The runtime corpus lacks supporting evidence. This is not equivalent to evidence of absence unless record-duty and access conditions justify it.

**Record-contingent claim:** A claim whose posterior depends materially on a missing, inaccessible, or controlled record.

**Institutional incentive profile:** Model of an actor’s role, record control, exposure, incentive to disclose, incentive to conceal, concealment penalty, and source reliability.

**SNO:** Structured Narrative Object: a claim/relation/evidence graph with provenance.

**GNWF:** Grounded Narrative Wave Function: wave/coherence representation with evidence gates.

**Chirality:** Mismatch between narrative states, especially language–logic round-trip distortion, evidence-weighted graph opposition, residual contradiction, or access-state mismatch.

**Access chirality:** Mismatch between a narrative’s implied record state and the structured record-access state.

**Orthesis:** Stable structured state that survives grounding/rendering; fixed point of `G(S(T,A))`.

**Multiverse view:** A ranked set of possible worlds/hypothesis states maintained under uncertainty.

**World view:** A specific candidate structured state containing facts, rules, assumptions, contexts, access hypotheses, proofs, and posterior mass.

**Zero-temperature rule:** A strict, proof-carrying tensor-logic rule approximating deterministic inference.

**Soft rule:** A rule that allows analogical, abductive, access-state, or probabilistic inference and cannot alone promote strict truth.

**Runtime oracle:** Any source of hidden gold labels or direct truth judgment used during deployment. Forbidden for undersupervised runs.

**Training oracle:** Labels or expert judgments used offline for training/calibration/evaluation. Allowed with disclosure.

**Residual tensor:** Tensor encoding unresolved support/refute contradiction mass.

**Latent context predicate:** Inferred context such as time, subgroup, source frame, mechanism, or measurement method that resolves contradiction.

**Access predicate:** Inferred or classified condition such as record expected but inaccessible, withheld, destroyed, not generated, or produced and non-supportive.

**ZTHR:** Zero-temperature hallucination rate: promoted strict claims without valid proof trace. Target is zero.
