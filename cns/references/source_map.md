# Source Map

## Internal CNS lineage used

The uploaded CNS corpus establishes the prior internal line: CNS 2.0 introduced Structured Narrative Objects, a multi-component critic pipeline, dialectical synthesis, and evidential entanglement. Later internal materials reframed CNS through grounded resonance, tensor logic, orthesis, and a language-logic bundle. This docset treats those as the internal design lineage and evolves them into a buildable CNS 7.0 / GCTS proposal.
